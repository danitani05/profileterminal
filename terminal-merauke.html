Terminal Overview

TPK Merauke
at a Glance

Terminal Petikemas Merauke (TPK Merauke) is the sole container terminal serving the easternmost city of Indonesia. Strategically positioned at the southern coast of Papua, it is the primary logistics gateway for Merauke and the broader South Papua province.

Under Pelindo Terminal Petikemas — a subsidiary of PT Pelabuhan Indonesia (Persero) / Danantara — TPK Merauke has achieved consistent double-digit throughput growth, reaching 52,715 TEUs in 2025 (+14% YoY), outperforming its RKAP target.

Hinterland & Connectivity

Shipping Routes & Trade Flows

TPK Merauke connects South Papua to the national supply chain via four dedicated shipping lines from Surabaya.

1
SPIL — Multi-stop Eastern Route
Surabaya → Makassar → Timika → Merauke
Primary inter-island container route linking South Papua to Sulawesi and East Java.
2
Tanto — Via Tual Loop
Surabaya → Makassar → Tual → Timika → Merauke
Extended eastern loop connecting Maluku archipelago ports.
3
Temas Direct — Express Service
Surabaya → Merauke (Direct)
Fastest supply line for time-sensitive cargo direct from East Java.
4
Temas — Via Agats
Surabaya → Agats → Timika → Merauke
Serves the Asmat regency before terminating at Merauke.

Strategic Outlook

Development & Growth Potential

TPK Merauke is at a critical inflection point. National strategic projects in Merauke signal an imminent cargo surge that requires immediate capacity preparation.

🌾 Merauke — National Strategic Project (PSN) Zone

Under Presidential Decree No. 15/2024, Merauke has been designated as a national food & bioethanol self-sufficiency task force area. Combined with the PSN Wanam industrial complex, TPK Merauke must prepare for a significant increase in container volumes in the coming years.

9,781 haAgricultural land cleared
5,000 m³HSD tank capacity (70% complete)

CY Expansion Options Under Evaluation

🏭
Option 1 — Pelindo Land Area
±Rp 249 Billion
  • HGB land owned by Pelindo — reduced acquisition risk
  • Located close to TPK Merauke facility
  • Access road: ±320 m required
  • Clay soil characteristics — special ground treatment needed
  • Social issues: residential relocation & drainage
🐟
Option 2 — Fisheries Agency Land
±Rp 192 Billion
  • Support from Fisheries Agency, ALFI/ILFA, KSOP
  • Clay soil — very unstable for CY operations
  • Access: 4 km from terminal — convoy escort required
  • Container routing from port 02:00–06:00 AM only
  • Requires road widening by Regional Government
🌊
Option 3 — Deck on Pile Extension
±Rp 365 Billion
  • Expansion within existing TPK footprint — no social issues
  • Operationally adjacent — zero external access needed
  • Physical works can begin immediately
  • Higher investment cost; KEMENHUB permit required
  • Comprehensive sedimentation & abrasion study needed
🤝

Priority Actions

  • CY rehabilitation for Lini 1 & Lini 2
  • Ideal equipment ratio: QCC, RTG & Side Loader
